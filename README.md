# Lane League

One lane, you against a CPU opponent, League-style, wrapped in a roguelike run. Built with Three.js, TypeScript and Vite.

**The run:** pick a champion (portraits along the top; the selected champion stands on the podium beside their full kit, ←/→ to browse, Enter to start), take a free starting gift (1 of 3 silver augments, then 1 of 3 **relics**: playful passives that are never sold in the shop: Ricochet Charm, Wisp Lantern, Phoenix Feather, Lucky Coin, Echo Bell, Leech Seed), and climb 5 floors (Rookie → Contender → Veteran → Champion → Legend). Each duel uses League's 1v1 rules: the first to **2 kills, 45 CS or a tower** wins (any champion death scores for the other side, tower executes included). Both champions start at level 3 with Q/E/W learned and earn double XP. After each win, pick 1 of 3 **augments** (silver after floor 1, gold after 2 and 3, prismatic before the boss; one reroll per run). Opponents bring their own augments, and their aim and stats scale with the floor.

**How to play:** "❔ How to play" on the home screen opens a guide (`src/ui/howToPlay.ts`): the duel's win conditions, the controls, a few tips and the ways to play.

**Bot vs bot:** "🤖 Watch bots duel" on the home screen picks two champions (or Random) and lets you spectate a duel between two Legend-level bots under the run's duel rules, at 1×, 2× or 4× speed (Esc or Leave to go back).

**Hero skills** (called summoners in the code): pick two on the champion select panel (D and F; hold to aim, release to cast) and swap them for free in the shop between floors: **Blink** (blink 4, works while rooted), **Scorch** (burn the enemy champion and halve their healing), **Ghost** (+35% move speed), **Ward** (a 2.5s shield), **Tether** (a skillshot that tags; cast again to dash to it, a miss costs only 20s), **Rage** (bonus attack damage and +35% attack speed). Cooldowns are 60–75s, so each comes up once or twice a duel. The CPU uses its champion's signature pair (shown on the floor intro). Free play takes `&spells=flash,ghost` (the code ids are older names: `flash` is Blink, `ignite` Scorch, `barrier` Ward). Code: `src/sim/summoners.ts`.

**Shop:** gold you earn in a duel, plus a 250 victory bonus, carries over. After each augment pick, a shop sells 5 seeded items, kept for the rest of the run (max 6). Basic items are 300–400 gold, mid items 900–1000 (e.g. Stormcaller Bow: every third attack calls lightning) and big items 1400–1500 (e.g. Tidebreaker Aegis: a shield when you drop low). It also sells next-duel consumables: Elixir of Wrath, Tome of Insight (start a level higher) and Fortune Token (+1 augment reroll). Opponents carry items from a budget of 800 gold per floor. Items live in `src/sim/items.ts` and share the augment hook system (`Perk`). Floors 2–5 each add a lane modifier (Rush Hour, Glass Towers, Bloodlust, Overcharged, Gold Rush). A loss ends the run. The run saves between floors (a reload resumes at the next intro or the pending augment pick), and best floors per champion are kept. Code: `src/run/run.ts` (floors, offers, saves), `src/sim/augments.ts`, `src/sim/rules.ts`, and `src/duel.ts` (one duel's world, views and input).

```bash
npm install
npm run dev        # http://localhost:5173 (?seed=42 for a fixed seed)
npm test           # sim tests
```

**Controls:** right-click the ground to move or an enemy to attack (hold to steer) · hold Q/W/E/R to aim and release to cast (right-click while aiming cancels) · Alt+Q/W/E/R or the + buttons to level an ability · S stop · Y lock/unlock camera (edge-pan when unlocked) · hold Space to centre the camera · wheel zoom · minimap (bottom right): left-click or drag to look there (unlocks the camera; Y or Space brings it back), right-click or drag to walk there. Free play (no run, nexus to win): `?champ=arlo|tock|maren|lisha|mabel|aurelle|gormund|sable`, `&enemy=…` (defaults to the other one), `&seed=42`, `&augments=brute,haste&items=i.blade` (comma-separated ids) to start with upgrades. Augments and items show as icon rows left of the ability bar, growing outward.

**Online 1v1 (PvP).** Start the server next to the game (`npm run server`, port 8787), then pick **⚔ Play online** on the home screen: one player creates a room and shares its 4-letter code, the other joins, both pick a champion and hero skills and ready up. The duel uses the normal duel rules (first to 2 kills, 45 CS or the first tower), both players get the recap, and the room stays open for a rematch. Leaving mid-duel forfeits. Tick **Relics** in the room and, once both players are ready, each chooses 1 of 3 random relics (the run's starting gifts) to carry into the duel; after 20 seconds one is picked for you. Either player can toggle it, and it applies to every rematch in the room.
- The server runs the only simulation at 30 Hz; clients send their commands and draw from a snapshot of the world each tick (`src/net/protocol.ts`), compressed to roughly 50 KB/s a player. A room (`server/room.ts`) is hosted either by the Node server (`server/main.ts`) or on Cloudflare (`worker/index.ts`, one Durable Object per room). Players connect to `/room/new` to create a room or `/room/CODE` to join; `/health` answers `ok`.
- By default the game looks for the server on the machine serving it (LAN: whoever runs `npm run dev` also runs `npm run server`). Point it elsewhere with `?server=wss://host:port` or `VITE_PVP_SERVER` at build time, for a hosted server.
- Your own walking is predicted (`src/net/predict.ts`): it moves the moment you click, and each snapshot reconciles it (commands are numbered and acknowledged; unacknowledged ones are replayed over one round trip). Casts, dashes and the other player come straight from the server. Your ping shows top-left. If the server goes quiet for 2 seconds a "Connection unstable" banner shows.
- **Drops:** if your connection closes (or nothing arrives for 6 seconds) the game reconnects to the same duel with the seat token it was given on joining. The server pauses the duel and holds the seat for 30 seconds, showing the other player "Opponent disconnected" with a countdown; back in time, the duel picks up where it paused. Otherwise it's a forfeit: a "Connection lost" screen for you, a win for them. The seat is also kept in the tab's session storage, so refreshing the page mid-duel rejoins it too (closing the tab doesn't).
- To feel lag on one machine: `LATENCY=100 npm run server` holds every message 100 ms each way.
- **Cloudflare:** one Worker serves both the game (the built `dist/`) and the PvP server, at https://lane-league.crevice.workers.dev. `npm run deploy` builds and deploys both (`npx wrangler login` once first). A build looks for its server on the site that served it; set `VITE_PVP_SERVER` to host the game somewhere else. `npm run server:cf` runs the Worker locally on port 8787 (run `npm run build` first for the game). Local `wrangler dev` logs "Network connection lost" whenever a player disconnects; the room handles it normally.
- Not yet: augments and items in PvP, matchmaking (it would pair players into a room instead of trading a code), and reconnecting after a dropped connection.

**Lisha, the Blossom Blade** (melee duelist, Irelia-like): **Petal Harvest** passive: consuming a mark with Q heals her · **Q Petal Step**: dash to a clicked enemy and cut it; resets if it was marked (also refunds half the mana) or dies · **W Find Your Center**: 1s meditation (rooted), then 3s of 35–55% damage reduction and 3% max HP/s regeneration · **E Twin Petals**: a pincer: two blades start beside her and close on the aimed point; everything inside the V is cut (outermost first) and marked (minions take 80%) · **R Blossom Storm**: a fan of 9 blades, up to 3 per enemy, marking everything.

**Mabel, the Beekeeper** (a mouse; short-range lane pusher, smallest hitbox): spends **bees** instead of mana (4 rising to 8, one regrows every 4s, last hits give one). **The Swarm** passive: each bee adds damage to her attacks, honeyed enemies take 30% more from her bees, minions that die stung count as her last hits (**Pollinated**), and getting hit by a champion makes her scurry (+30% speed). **Q Sting**: a bee latches on and stings over 3s; stings stack · **W Honey Pot**: a slowing puddle that honeys enemies · **E Hive**: a 10s hive that fires bees, buffs nearby allied minions and doubles her bee regrowth · **R Queen's Fury**: refill the swarm and dive one champion with every bee.

**Pip & Thistle, the Odd Couple** (experimental; placeholder models): a tiny sprout-knight riding a giant, anxious toad. **Best Friends** passive: Thistle is a second unit (a *pet*) with his own health that grows with Pip's level. While Pip rides, damage hits Thistle first; at 0 Thistle panics, throws Pip off and bounds home (untargetable) for 12s, coming back at half health. On foot, Thistle bites whatever Pip attacks, snaps at a champion attacking Pip, stops skillshots with his body, and minions and towers go for him before Pip. His kills are Pip's; only Pip's death counts. Riding (melee, faster, +20% auto damage): **Q Gulp** tongue skillshot (minions take 150%; one it finishes is swallowed and heals Thistle; the enemy champion is yanked in), **W Hop** leap with a slowing splash, **E Hop Off** vault off (+20% move speed for 2s) and fight on foot. On foot (5-range slingshot): **Q Pebble Burst** three pebbles in a spread (repeat hits on one target deal 50%), **W Puff Up** Thistle hops to a spot and puffs up for 3s (40% less damage taken, slows enemies around him; he doesn't grow), **E Saddle Up** dash to Thistle and climb on. Each form keeps its own Q/W cooldowns. **R Big Leap** (both forms): Thistle crashes onto an area, damaging and knocking up (0.8s stun) everyone there; riding, Pip comes along. **Aurelle, the Spellspear** (floating battle mage): **Bound Weapon** passive: holding her spear she attacks with 2.6-range thrusts; without it, arcane bolts (range 5, 70% damage); picking it up empowers her next thrust, and a dropped spear flies back after 6s · **Q Arcane Lance**: throw the spear through minions; if it impales a champion it stops in them and she can recast within 3s to rip it back through everything (60% damage); a miss lands at max range and can't be recalled · **W Glyph Step**: blink, leaving a glyph that detonates and slows · **E Sweep / Arcane Nova**: with the spear, a knockback sweep; without it, a bigger burst · **R Arsenal Ascendant**: for 6s, Arcane Lance throws three returning spectral lances (2s cooldown) and her attacks cleave; then they crash down around her.

**Gormund, Lord of the Labyrinth** (a minotaur; melee juggernaut, bare-handed): no mana; **abilities cost a share of his current health** (never lethal). **Thick Hide** passive: after 3s without trading with the enemy champion or taking tower shots (minions don't count), he regenerates 5% max HP per second; **Fury**: up to +30% damage as his health drops, maxed at 30% health · **Q Hurl Stone** (7%): a rock skillshot that slows 35% and shatters for 60% splash; hitting the champion refunds its cost · **W Bellow** (5%): a roar that slows nearby enemies 35%, gives him 25% speed for 2s and 25% damage reduction for 3s · **E Bull Rush** (10%): a straight 6-unit charge; the first champion in the way is carried to the end and stunned for 0.75s, minions are shoved aside · **R Labyrinth Rage**: for 7s he grows huge, is unstoppable (sheds and ignores slows, roots, stuns and pulls), casts for free and heals for 20% of his damage.

**Sable, the Silent** (a quiet harvest reaper; melee catcher with a chained scythe): **Gleaning** passive: hooked enemies are Gleaned for 4s (she reaches 1.2 further against them, and her damage to them heals her 25%); her attacks sweep, hitting others in a short arc for 50% · **Q Reaping Hook**: a skillshot that yanks the first enemy to her and opens a 2.5s **Reaping window** for ONE follow-up (autos in the window are ordinary, so weave some in first): **Q again** to *Reap* (an empowered cone swing, up to 75% stronger the lower they are), **W** to *Bind* (root 1.25s), or **E** to *Vault* (leap over them to their far side and slow them). The follow-ups glow as recasts on Q, W and E while the window is open, and using one closes it · **W Wide Swath**: a slowing spin (extra damage to minions) · **E Drift**: a short dash; she takes 25% less damage for 2s · **R Last Harvest**: after a 0.5s wind-up (telegraphed), a great crescent drags everything in it to her and opens the window on all of them.

Marked units (Lisha's marks, Arlo's Rune Flare) wear a pulsing ring and a floating petal in the marker's colour.

Crowd control: **root** (no walking, dashing or blinking), **stun** (nothing, and it interrupts wind-ups), **shields** (absorb damage first, shown white on health bars) and **pulls**. **Unstoppable** units ignore all of it. A cast pressed during another wind-up or dash is buffered for 0.4s, so combos like E→W don't drop keys.

Levels 1–11 from XP (minions dying within 12 units, champion kills). Each level gives a skill point: basics up to rank ceil(level/2), max 5; ultimate at 6 and 11.

- **Lane rules:** a wave of 3 melee + 3 caster minions every 30 s (first at 0:03). Minions fight minions, then structures, then champions. Towers shoot minions before champions, unless you hit their champion while in range (and nearby enemy minions switch to you too). The tower behind is invulnerable until the one in front falls, and the nexus until both have. Last hits pay gold (melee 21, caster 14, champion 300, tower 150). Champions respawn after 8 s and heal fast at their fountain behind the nexus. Destroy the nexus to win (R to play again). Numbers live in `src/sim/stats.ts`.
- `src/sim/` holds the deterministic game simulation (no three.js). `src/render/` draws it, and `src/main.ts` wires them together.
- `docs/plan.md` covers milestones and the asset list. `docs/art-bible.md` covers the look and the prompt blocks.
- **Asset pipeline** (copied from `threejs-roguelike`): `npm run meshy -- <cmd>` (concept, model, remesh, rig, animate; needs `MESHY_API_KEY` in `.env.local`). Then run `Blender -b --python tools/blender/import_meshy.py -- assets-src/meshy/<name>/import.json` to build the game GLB in `public/assets/meshy/`.
